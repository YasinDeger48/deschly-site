English

Deschly does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the app does not use a server.

Your progress, saved words, notes and quiz results are stored only on your device. A backup file is created and shared only by you.

German speech uses the iPhone's built-in speech synthesis on the device. Daily reminders are scheduled locally by your iPhone. Video links open in YouTube, where YouTube's own privacy policy applies.
